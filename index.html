Frequently Asked Questions

What is a freelance rate?

A freelance rate is the amount a freelancer charges for their professional time or services. It can be hourly, daily, project-based or another pricing structure.

Why shouldn't I simply divide my desired income by working hours?

Freelancers also have business expenses, taxes, unpaid administrative time, holidays and other costs. This calculator helps you account for those factors.

Should I use every working hour as billable?

No. Freelancers normally spend some time on proposals, communication, administration, marketing and other non-billable work.

Can I change the currency?

Yes. Select your preferred currency before calculating your freelance rate.

Is this calculator financial advice?

No. The results are estimates for planning and informational purposes only. Consider your personal circumstances and professional advice where appropriate.
